## 📊 Project Overview

**Mumbai Retail Sales Analysis** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze retail sales performance across different areas of Mumbai.

The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of **sales, orders, quantity, customers, products, categories, discounts, and location-wise performance**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall retail sales performance
* Track monthly sales and order trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Compare sales performance across categories
* Analyze location-wise sales performance
* Monitor important business KPIs
* Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks the following KPIs:

| KPI                        | Description               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 💰 **Total Sales**         | Overall sales revenue     |
| 🧾 **Total Orders**        | Total number of orders    |
| 📦 **Total Quantity**      | Total units sold          |
| 👥 **Total Customers**     | Total customers           |
| 🏷️ **Average Discount**   | Average discount provided |
| 🛒 **Average Order Value** | Average value per order   |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 📅 Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzes sales performance over time and helps identify monthly sales patterns.

### 🛍️ Sales by Category

Compares sales performance across different product categories.

### 🏆 Top 5 Products by Sales

Identifies the top five products based on sales performance.

### 📦 Monthly Order Trend

Shows how the number of orders changes over time.

### 🥧 Sales Contribution by Category

Shows the contribution of different categories to total sales.

### 📍 Sales by Location

Provides a geographical view of sales across different areas of Mumbai.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

* 📅 **Order Date**
* 📍 **Area / Location**

Users can select different dates and locations to dynamically explore the sales data.
